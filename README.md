### 🤝 CSD Affiliate Sales Program
For anyone with relationships in engineering, manufacturing, or digital who
wants to earn without running projects. **You bring the customer, we handle
everything else.**

1. **You have a customer** — a potential or existing lead.
2. **Submit their info** through the affiliate form (auto-sent to the company).
3. **The CSD team makes contact** — confirms needs and sets up a shared channel
   (customer + partner + company). You stay updated on progress.
4. **We deliver the project** — our engineering and digital teams execute
   professionally, on quality and on schedule. No technical work on your side.
5. **You get paid** — transparent commission, paid on time, with clear reporting.

Commission scales with deal size across CAD/engineering projects, digital
services, and academy courses. No capital, no team, no KPI pressure — just your
network plus our delivery system. **Win – Win – Win.**

## Founder's story

**Mr. Pham Tien Phong** is a mechanical engineer with hands-on experience at
leading corporations in the heavy industry and food processing sectors. Working
across machine design, technical documentation, and real-world manufacturing, he
saw first-hand the gap between engineering concepts and practical execution.

He founded **CADSQUAD Vietnam** with a clear mission: to deliver reliable,
high-quality technical solutions while empowering the next generation of
engineers and digital creators. His vision is to build more than a service
company — a **trusted technical ecosystem** where innovation, education, and
digital transformation turn ideas into real-world, sustainable solutions.
